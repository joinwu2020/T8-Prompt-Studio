# 柯达 2383 院线拷贝质感 (Kodak Vision 2383 Print Look)

柯达 2383 是电影放映用的正片：负片（如 Vision3 500T）洗印到它上面才进影院，决定了“银幕上最终的颜色”。调色界常说的“2383 LUT / 印片模拟”就是它。

气质：**干净、厚重、高级的现代院线胶片味**，不是老旧褪色。与昭和 1960 / 1970 老拷贝模板是两套美学，不要混用。

适用：MiniMax H3 全参考 / T2VA 格式。人物、剧情另写，本模板只管画质、光影、声音。

## 一、画面特征

- 对比：明显的胶片 S 曲线，中间调饱满；黑位深而浓，但暗部仍有细节（不是死黑）
- 高光：过渡极柔和，接近过曝时缓缓滚到暖奶白，不会数码削顶
- 色彩：高光暖金琥珀，暗部偏青绿/蓝绿——克制的“暖高光、冷阴影”
- 肤色：浓郁、偏暖、有密度，红润不发橙
- 饱和：红橙浓，绿偏青，蓝深沉；饱满但不艳俗
- 颗粒：细、有机、均匀，暗部略明显
- 拷贝状态：全新，无划痕、灰尘、闪烁、褪色

## 二、完整风格句（detailed_description 第一句）

```text
The target video has the look of a modern theatrical film release shot on Kodak Vision3 negative and printed on Kodak Vision 2383 color print film, NOT a clean digital grade. Contrast: a rich, filmic S-curve with dense, deep blacks that still hold soft shadow detail, full-bodied mid-tones, and highlights that roll off smoothly and gently into warm cream, never clipping harshly. Color: warm golden-amber highlights against cool teal-cyan shadows, dense, warm, natural skin tones with healthy red-pink depth, deep saturated reds and oranges, greens shifted slightly toward cyan, deep inky blues, and an overall saturated yet restrained palette. Texture: fine, organic, evenly distributed film grain, slightly more visible in the shadows, with gentle optical softness rather than digital sharpness. Finish: a clean, polished, freshly struck theatrical print with no scratches, dust, flicker or fading.
```

## 三、光线补充句（任选一句，接在风格句后）

**日间室内窗光**
```text
Soft daylight from the window falls as a warm key, with the room's shadows sinking into cool teal, a natural 3:1 lighting ratio.
```

**夜间钨丝灯 / 室内暖灯**
```text
A single warm tungsten practical lamp provides the key light, its glow rendered as rich amber on the skin, while the unlit parts of the room fall into deep teal-blue shadow, a 4:1 lighting ratio.
```

**夜间街景**
```text
Warm sodium and neon practicals glow amber and red against a deep cyan-blue night, with the darkest areas falling into dense, inky blacks that still hold faint detail.
```

**户外白天**
```text
Bright natural sunlight renders warm, golden highlights on skin and surfaces, with cool cyan-blue in the open shade and sky, and soft, smooth highlight roll-off in the brightest areas.
```

## 四、皮肤与高光防坑句（强烈建议必加）

2383 对比高、饱和浓，MiniMax 上极易油亮、过曝、过饱和。用这句压住：

```text
Skin stays dry and matte with natural pores and a soft, velvety finish, with no oily shine or specular hotspots. The brightest areas, such as windows and lamps, stay bright but still visible with their detail intact, and faces are evenly exposed.
```

## 五、极简关键词版（怕长句稀释权重时放最前）

```text
Kodak 2383 print film look, deep dense blacks, filmic S-curve contrast, warm amber highlights, cool teal shadows, rich warm skin, fine film grain, smooth highlight roll-off
```

## 六、声音（overall_soundscape）

2383 是现代院线新拷贝，声音应干净：

```text
Clean, full-bodied cinema sound with natural room tone and clear, close dialogue, warm and slightly rounded rather than crisp and digital.
```

想加一点老片味：
```text
Warm, slightly narrow theatrical sound with a very faint hiss.
```

## 七、调节旋钮

| 想要的效果 | 怎么改 |
|---|---|
| 更厚重、更暗 | 加 `slightly underexposed, moody low-key` |
| 对比更柔 | `rich, filmic S-curve` → `gentle, filmic S-curve` |
| 青橙感更强 | 加 `strong teal-and-orange separation` |
| 青橙感更弱、更自然 | `cool teal-cyan shadows` → `slightly cool neutral shadows` |
| 颗粒更明显（500T 夜景） | `fine` → `visible, slightly coarse Vision3 500T grain` |
| 加一点年代感 | 句末加 `a faint projection softness and very subtle gate weave` |

## 八、常见坑

- 不写 high contrast / vivid / HDR / sharp：会变数码高对比，油亮俗艳。用 filmic S-curve、rich、dense 代替。
- 不写 crushed blacks：会压成死黑丢细节。2383 的黑是“浓”，不是“死”。
- teal and orange 别写太重：会变成好莱坞滤镜感，2383 的冷暖分离很克制。
- 主光只写一个：多光源会被模型打平，冷暖对比出不来。
- 别和老片模板混用：2383 是干净新拷贝，加划痕、褪色、抬黑就成四不像。要旧请用昭和 1960 或 1970 模板。
- 加速 LoRA 步数太少（如 3 步）也会造成油腻、过饱和、高对比，至少 4 步。

## 九、用法步骤

1. detailed_description 第一句：完整风格句（或极简版放最前）。
2. 按场景接一句光线补充句。
3. 必加皮肤与高光防坑句。
4. overall_soundscape 套声音模板。
5. 效果不对时按“调节旋钮”逐项微调，一次只改一项。
